Who Owns Tower? · What we don't know

The open questions

Everything the records reviewed so far do not establish, what document would settle each one, and where we are on getting it. This page is kept current.

Last updated October 21, 2026

An investigation that lists what it hasn't proved is harder to attack than one that doesn't. This is that list. When a question is answered, the answer moves to the page where it belongs and the entry here says so.

Ownership

  • Who holds the equity in any Tower company. California lists managers; Florida lists authorized members; neither lists percentages. A managerial title does not establish ownership, and a listed member is a disclosed role without a stated share. No filing reviewed states who owns Tower Mobility, Tower EV, Tower LA, CarToo, or the Florida entities. What would settle it: operating agreements, capitalization tables, or a filed exhibit naming members and shares. Not public unless litigated. In progress
  • Whether the Munoz brothers own any part of the Tzur companies, or Tzur any part of theirs. The 2023 Florida venture is the only filing that puts both sides in one entity, and it lasted four weeks; its dissolution certificate says only that the business was "no longer viable economically." What would settle it: any agreement among the 2023 members, which would be private. The public filings on both sides are now in hand.
  • Daryl Hagler's role. He appears as a member of Tower WAV LLC (2022 index), a member of the 2023 Florida venture, and the first registered agent of Tower Mobility LLC. Nothing in the record says whether he invested, what he owned, or whether he is still involved. What would settle it: the original California filings for Tower WAV LLC and Tower Mobility LLC, including statements of information.
  • Spectrum.ai LLC. AVMobility's Florida registration originally listed SPECTRUM.AI LLC as its member; a June 8, 2026 amendment replaced it with Andres Munoz. We have not identified what Spectrum.ai is, where it is organized, or who controls it. A website with the same name is not evidence of a connection. What would settle it: AVMobility's Wyoming articles and amendment history, and Spectrum.ai's own formation record by state and number.

Money

  • Whether any Queens entity finances Tower's fleet. No verified debtor-specific UCC search result has been retrieved; web-indexed searches turned up nothing attributable, and the state registries' interactive search was not completed, so this is an access limitation, not a negative finding. Titled vehicles are also often secured through title liens that never appear in a UCC index. What would settle it: debtor-exact UCC searches for Tower EV, Tower Mobility, Tower WAV, WAV Mobile, CarToo, Tower LA, Tower WAV FL, and AVMobility, in California, Florida, and Wyoming, plus any title-lien records. In progress
  • The lending license. A Tower Group executive's biography says she obtained a California Financing Law license. We have not established the legal licensee, license number, issuance date, or status from the public records available to us. The claim is attributed to her and unverified; failure to identify a license proves neither that none exists nor that any company is operating unlicensed. What would settle it: a DFPI or NMLS record under the exact legal name of the licensee, or a DFPI certificate of search.
  • Who paid for the Rivians, and where they are. No VIN, title, lien, or shipment record. What would settle it: a UCC or title-lien filing with a collateral schedule, or a Florida vehicle registration.

CarToo

  • What an executed CarToo contract says. CarToo's general terms publish that late payment can mean fees, lockout, or termination, and that cars shut off for non-payment. The weekly rate, term, total cost, buyout, mileage cap, title conditions, and the notice and procedure before a shutoff are not published. What would settle it: a signed agreement from a driver. We are asking.
  • Who holds title to CarToo's cars, and whether any came from Tower's fleet.
  • Whether Tower drivers are referred to CarToo. Nothing in the record shows a referral practice. We don't claim one.
  • Christopher Bjelkstam, CarToo's second manager. Nothing else in the record.

Miami

  • Any connection between the Queens businesses and the Miami address. None found. No shared officer, agent, lease, deed, lien, or vendor record links Tzur, Tsour, Tower Auto Mall, CB Livery, Tower LA, or CarToo to the Kvachuks, MKAB Holdings, OnlyVans, or 3700 NW 27th Avenue. Tower's separate connections to each side do not connect the two sides to each other.
  • Whether a Tower lease, permit, or operation ever existed at 3700 NW 27th Avenue. See Tower's Miami Paper Trail.

A note on an address discrepancy

The FMCSA carrier snapshot for Tower EV LLC (USDOT 4315890) shows a physical address of "38000 N Mission Rd," with an extra zero. Every other record uses 3800. We read it as a data-entry error in the federal record and note it here rather than silently correcting it or treating it as a second facility.
