Fleet Watch · Who Owns Tower?

If you drive for Tower, read this before you sign anything else at Mission Road.

What public records show about the companies behind your paycheck, the car-rental business on your lot, and the New York leasing network both lead back to.

Research cutoff October 21, 2026 · Documented filings, court records, company pages · Employee-reported where marked

Your employer of record is Tower EV LLC; that's on your pay stub. Florida's corporate filing for Tower EV lists Daniel Munoz as its manager. What isn't on your pay stub is that the lot you park on is also the business address of a rent-to-own car company called CarToo, which rents cars by the week to rideshare drivers earning at least $700 a week, which is about what a full week at Tower pays. CarToo's manager is the president of a used-car and livery dealership in Queens, New York, whose weekly-payment leases to Uber drivers were documented by Quartz in 2017 (one driver: $495 a week for 159 weeks, with the car remotely disabled when he fell behind, by his account) and described by a federal judge in 2025 as a business that "only provides loans for the purchase of its own goods." CarToo's own published terms say late payment "may result in additional fees, vehicle lockout, or termination."

The public record does not say how that business and the Munoz brothers' business are connected financially. It does say the two sides were named together in a Florida company in 2023, and share a depot today. Below is everything the filings show, with links to every source, and a plain list of what they don't show. If anyone at Mission Road offers you a weekly-payment car, read every page of the agreement before you sign it. Then send us a copy.

What the records show, in five lines

  1. Two management chains, one address. At 3800 N. Mission Road, the filings list the Munoz brothers as managers of Tower Mobility, Tower WAV, and Tower EV, and Ariel Tzur, president of Tower Auto Mall in Queens, as manager of Tower LA and CarToo. A managerial title does not establish ownership, and a manager can also be an owner; the filings don't say. The entities.
  2. They have been on a filing together since 2023. A Florida company formed in May 2023, with Daniel Munoz as registered agent, listed a "Ralph Tsour" as a member, at the Queens dealership's address; Tower Auto Mall's CEO is listed elsewhere as Rafael Tsour, and the address is the basis for treating them as probably the same person. It was dissolved a month later as "no longer viable economically"; a similarly named company with Andres Munoz as its only member took effect the day before. The 2023 Florida filings.
  3. The Queens business is a subprime vehicle lender for Uber drivers. That's what Quartz reported in 2017 and what a federal court described in 2025. Tzur is the manager of the leasing company in the 2017 case, by his own sworn affidavit. The Queens business.
  4. CarToo publishes a lockout policy but no prices. Its terms say late payment can mean fees, "vehicle lockout," or termination, and its text-message terms mention shutoff warnings for non-payment. The weekly rate, term, total cost, and buyout are only in the contract each driver signs. CarToo.
  5. Tower began as an Uber wheelchair-van contractor in 2020. Uber's own regulatory filings document the contract (October 2020) and the launch (December 2020). The operating history predates the 2023 Florida venture; it does not establish whether any interests were later bought or sold. Origins.

What the records don't show

Who owns what. A manager's or agent's title does not establish ownership, a listed member is a disclosed role without a stated share, and no filing we reviewed states a percentage. Whether a Queens entity finances Tower's fleet. Which company holds the lending license a Tower Group executive says she obtained. What a CarToo contract says. Who or what "Spectrum.ai LLC," the member that AVMobility's Florida filing originally listed, actually is. And anything that connects any of this to the Miami address. The full list, kept current.
