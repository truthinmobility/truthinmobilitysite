Who Owns Tower? · The 2023 Florida filings

Four weeks in Sarasota

In May 2023, a Florida company put a Munoz and a Tower Auto Mall principal on the same filing. It lasted 28 days. What replaced it listed only a Munoz.

Research cutoff October 21, 2026 · Documented Florida Division of Corporations entries

This is the only filing we have found that places the Munoz brothers' business and the Queens leasing business inside one legal entity. It is also a filing that was undone almost as soon as it was made. Both facts matter, and the record explains neither.

The first company

Documented Tower WAV Florida LLC, Florida document L23000262938, was filed May 30, 2023, effective May 25. Its principal address was 5911 Midnight Pass Road, Unit 103, Sarasota. Its registered agent was Daniel Munoz. It listed three authorized members:

  • WAV Mobile LLC, a California company formed in July 2020 and associated in registry records with the Munoz brothers.
  • Ralph Tsour, at 31-10 Queens Boulevard, Long Island City. That is the address of Tower Auto Mall, whose BBB record names Rafael Tsour as CEO. The filing says "Ralph"; the BBB says "Rafael"; the address is what connects them.
  • Daryl Hagler, who appears in a 2022 index of California records as a member of Tower WAV LLC, and in 2024 as the original registered agent of Tower Mobility LLC.

The entity had no federal employer ID on file. On June 27, 2023, it was voluntarily dissolved.

The second company

Documented Tower WAV FL LLC, document L23000310457, was filed on that same June 27, with an effective date of June 26, the day before the first company's dissolution was recorded. It used the same Sarasota street address. It lists one person: Andres Munoz, managing member. It carries its own FEIN (99-1421176), has filed annual reports in 2024, 2025, and 2026, and is active. Its May 2026 report changed the registered agent to a commercial service in Fort Lauderdale, which is an agent update, not evidence the business moved.

What that sequence does and doesn't establish

The overlapping dates, the shared address, and the near-identical name make a coordinated handoff the most natural reading: one company wound down and another, with a different set of names, took its place. We say that much. We do not say what it meant, because the public record doesn't. A Florida LLC summary is not a capitalization table. The disappearance of Tsour's and Hagler's names from the public listing shows a change in who was publicly listed. It does not show a buyout, a falling-out, an asset transfer, or that either man gave up an economic interest. It is equally consistent with a corrected filing, a changed plan, or a restructuring whose terms live in a private agreement.

Two things would settle it, and both are public documents we have not yet retrieved: the original articles and the June 27 dissolution certificate for L23000262938, which name whoever authorized the dissolution, and the original articles for L23000310457. In progress

Why it matters anyway

Because it dates the relationship. Until this filing surfaced, the earliest documented link between the Munoz operation and the Queens business was CarToo's formation at Mission Road in June 2025. This moves it back two years, to a moment when the two sides were willing to put their names on one piece of paper. Whatever the arrangement became, it did not begin with a car-rental company appearing on a Los Angeles lot. It began with a joint company in Florida.

It also shows which side the Florida idea belonged to. Every later Florida filing, Tower WAV LLC's 2025 registration, Tower EV in April 2026, AVMobility in May 2026, lists only a Munoz. Whether that is because the Queens side withdrew, or because its interest moved somewhere the public filings don't reach, is on the unknowns page.
